# Research notes

The original research notes have been superseded by the larger 0.2 fidelity
study in [FIDELITY-RESEARCH.md](FIDELITY-RESEARCH.md).

The key conclusion is that "Matrix rain" should not be treated as one generic
visual effect.  The 0.2 default explicitly targets the first-film operator CRT
screen language: a stable dense grid, tall narrow symbols, illumination waves
rather than moving text, flatter luminous bodies, isolated bright cursor cells,
4:3 framing and a restrained late-1990s display treatment.

No film bitmap, official font, promotional SWF glyph, or another project's
glyph texture is included.  Public references were used to infer visual rules;
the atlas remains the project's original clean-room artwork.
